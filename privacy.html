Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Entre Nous is a deck of conversation cards. It is made to be used around a table, not to watch you. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no login.
  • There is no advertising and no tracking. The only thing that can leave your phone is an anonymous thumbs up or down on a card, and you can switch that off.
  • Everything else you choose in the app stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

To remember your choices between visits, the app saves a few settings on your device only:

  • the language of the cards,
  • the questions you have saved with the heart,
  • the decks you have chosen,
  • the cards you have marked with a thumb,
  • how many cards you have played, so the daily reminder is offered only once,
  • your preferences: swipe sound, haptics, the daily reminder and its time, and the anonymous feedback switch.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or your computer, it is included in that backup, under Apple's own privacy terms. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Anonymous card feedback

Each card has a thumbs up and a thumbs down. When you tap one, and “Share anonymous feedback” is on in Settings, the app sends a short note so we can see which questions work and write better ones. It contains only:

  • the question’s number and its deck,
  • the language of the cards,
  • whether it was a thumbs up, a thumbs down, or a thumb taken back,
  • the version of the app.

It does not contain your name, your email, an account, or any device or advertising identifier. Every note gets a new random number, so two notes can’t be linked to each other or to you. Nothing is sent until you tap a thumb.

The notes are received by PostHog, our analytics provider, on its servers in the European Union, and the project is set to discard IP addresses. Because a note can’t identify anyone, it is anonymous information.

The switch is on when you first open the app. You can turn it off at any time in Settings, under Privacy. Then nothing is sent, and the thumbs still work for you on your phone.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, iOS asks whether Entre Nous may send you notifications. The reminders are scheduled by the app on your iPhone. No server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in the iPhone Settings.

Sharing a card

When you share a question, the app hands the text to the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes; we never see it.

Contacting support

If you write to us from the app, your email app opens with our address. We receive only what you choose to send, such as your email address and your message, and use it only to answer you. We do not add you to any list and we delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed.

Ratings

If you rate the app, the rating is handled by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy. We see only the anonymous ratings and the reviews you choose to publish.

Children

Entre Nous is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on our website, with a new effective date. If we ever wanted to collect anything more, we would ask you first.
