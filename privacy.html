Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Entre Nous is a deck of conversation cards. It is made to be used around a table, not to watch you. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no login.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • Everything you choose in the app stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

To remember your choices between visits, the app saves a few settings on your device only:

  • the language of the cards,
  • the questions you have saved with the heart,
  • the decks you have chosen,
  • your preferences: swipe sound, haptics, and the daily reminder and its time.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or your computer, it is included in that backup, under Apple's own privacy terms. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, iOS asks whether Entre Nous may send you notifications. The reminders are scheduled by the app on your iPhone. No server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in the iPhone Settings.

Sharing a card

When you share a question, the app hands the text to the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes; we never see it.

Contacting support

If you write to us from the app, your email app opens with our address. We receive only what you choose to send, such as your email address and your message, and use it only to answer you. We do not add you to any list and we delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed.

Ratings

If you rate the app, the rating is handled by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy. We see only the anonymous ratings and the reviews you choose to publish.

Children

Entre Nous is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on our website, with a new effective date. If we ever wanted to collect data, we would ask you first.
